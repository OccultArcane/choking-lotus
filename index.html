[ 03 / THE ATELIER ]

Beauty is a thing
that drowns slowly.

Choking Lotus is a small IMVU atelier carving couture for avatars that were never meant to look human. Every seam is placed by hand. Every reflection is earned.

We work in short tides — nine pieces, one silhouette, no reprints. The chrome is tarnished on purpose. The hems are weighted to move like wet silk. What looks delicate is built to hold its shape in every pose.

01

Sculpted, not generated

Original mesh, modelled petal by petal and drip by drip.

02

Fitted for IMVU

Rigged to the classic and deluxe bodies. Try-on variants included.

03

Small tides only

Limited runs, signed to the ledger. When it closes, it closes.
